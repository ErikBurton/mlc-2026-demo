About Hayley

Hayley has studied voice and music in depth since childhood, and her devotion to the craft grew tenfold when she began teaching in 2021. Her curriculum draws on tried-and-true methods like the Kodály method, mindfulness exercises and the Alexander Technique to develop healthy projection, musicianship and performance skills across genres.

Every singer has different needs, interests and vocal anatomy, so Hayley shapes each week's lesson around the student. No one leaves a lesson unprepared or unsure of how to practice. She also works in songwriting, studio recording, music production and stage performance, helping singers become well-rounded musicians.

She loves working with young singers of all ages and also teaches adults at beginner to intermediate levels. Hayley studied Music Industry Studies at Cal Poly Pomona.
