About David

Since 2005, Mr. Mount has been the District Accompanist for Upland Unified School District, playing for all of its choral programs, rehearsals, festivals, concerts, events and tours. He is also the accompanist for two San Bernardino high school choral programs, and he plays keyboard in the orchestra pit for college and professional theater productions throughout Southern California.

Being a professional musician has been a joy and an honor, and he loves sharing that experience with students of all ages and levels. Students gain practical knowledge of musical ideas, expression, styles and genres, along with theory, ear training, improvisation and much more.
