About

The first, and still the best

Mike Freeland and David Bown started Music Lessons Central in 1997 on Hospitality Lane in San Bernardino. In 2009 they moved into a much larger studio in Redlands, where they still teach today.

Call, email or stop by anytime for a tour and to learn about all the musical options available to you.
